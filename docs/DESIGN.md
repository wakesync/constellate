## Design Context

### Users
Constellate is used by small, trusted circles who want to understand themselves and one another through astrology, Human Design, Gene Keys, Enneagram, Socionics, convergence, and shared writing. They often open it on a phone, in the middle of an actual friendship, household, family, or collaboration. The job is not prediction. It is making computed patterns legible without turning people into diagnoses or relationships into compatibility scores.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, intimate, discerning. The voice is an old friend reading a letter twice: warm but not flattering, mystical without woo, honest without prescription. The interface should create calm attention and trust, especially around crown-jewel private data.

### Aesthetic Direction
Mobile-first parchment and illuminated-manuscript restraint. Cream parchment (#f5ecd9), ink (#1f1a14), oxblood (#7a2e2a), aged gold (#a98a3d), Cormorant Garamond with Inter. Fine rules, hand-drawn celestial motifs, flat paper cards, and typographic hierarchy. Deepen the existing aesthetic. Do not replace it with generic SaaS settings, glassmorphism, gradients, oversized hero type, or dense dashboards.

### Design Principles
1. Function before poetry: every action is understandable in plain language within two taps, then the voice may add atmosphere.
2. Private by construction: clearly label what is private, never expose intimate fields through social surfaces, and make consent legible without alarmism.
3. One meaningful choice at a time: use compact, contextual controls instead of settings-form slogs.
4. Deterministic truth, narrative restraint: computed evidence is primary; prose mirrors it and never claims fate.
5. Mobile touch first: 44px targets, readable serif measures, resilient wrapping, reduced-motion respect, and no information hidden behind hover.
