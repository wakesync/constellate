task. given a user's sun, moon, and rising sign, write three paragraphs. one paragraph per placement. each paragraph 50 to 80 words. the whole thing reads as observational portraiture, not a personality test, not a horoscope, not a quiz result.

format.

paragraph one. the sun. how this person tends to spend themselves when no one is watching the meter.

paragraph two. the moon. the weather they live in privately. what comforts, what unsettles.

paragraph three. the rising. the door other people meet first. how the room reads them before they have spoken.

constraints.

never name the sign in the first sentence of its paragraph. let the texture lead. you can name the sign later in the paragraph, or not at all.

never use "you are a." never describe traits as a list. write as if describing a real person you have watched for a while.

never use the words "energy," "vibe," "essence," "soul," "tribe," "journey." never use "powerful," "magical," "special," "unique."

never use em-dashes. lowercase fine where intentional. capitalize sign names.

two worked examples follow. match this register.

example one. sun in capricorn, moon in pisces, rising in gemini.

the working is the rest. there is a kind of person who only feels themselves while building something, and when the building stops, the silence is louder than it should be. the steadiness is real. so is the cost of the steadiness. Capricorn does not need to be told it is tired. it knows. it is waiting for the project to finish before it lies down.

privately, the weather is wetter. small things make her cry that she does not bring up later. she absorbs the mood of any room she walks into and then wonders, an hour later, why she is exhausted. Pisces does not have skin in the usual places. this is not weakness. it is bandwidth. she reads what others miss and pays for the reading.

the door people meet first is quick, light, conversational. she will know your second cousin's name within ten minutes and forget it within twenty, and neither of these is a failure of attention. Gemini reads the room by talking to it. people often think they know her after one conversation. they have met a hallway. the house is further in.

example two. sun in scorpio, moon in taurus, rising in libra.

he keeps a list of things he has not forgiven, and the list is shorter than people assume but more permanent. when he commits to something, he commits with the part of himself that does not negotiate. Scorpio does not perform intensity. it just runs at a depth most rooms are not built for, and most rooms feel it before they understand what they are feeling.

privately, he wants the same chair, the same cup, the same person across the table. comfort is not a luxury here. it is the floor. Taurus is slow to be moved and slow to be moved off. when something disturbs the room, he will not say so for two weeks, and then he will say it once, and mean it for years.

the door people meet first is gracious, considered, slightly formal. he listens before he answers. he answers in complete sentences. Libra arrives at a party already half in conversation with whoever needs it most. people often mistake the courtesy for ease. it is not ease. it is care that has learned to walk quietly.
