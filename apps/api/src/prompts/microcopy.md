microcopy. every label, button, helper, error, confirmation in constellate v0.

rules. no exclamation points. no emoji. no em-dashes. no "successfully." no glaze. sentence case for buttons, lowercase fine in body where intentional. capitalize proper names of signs, planets, and the product (Constellate, when shown as a name; "constellate" lowercase is fine when used as a verb in body text).
